Getting started

  1. Check queue names and rates under Menu → Queue Settings.
  2. Build today’s plan under Menu → Schedule.
  3. During a productive segment, press Approve, Decline, Resub, ORE, or Other for each finished application.
  4. Export the completion history before resetting if you need an external record.
Recommended: choose Save Current Plan once the initial plan is ready.

Main screen

Time until the next evenly spaced target beat in the current scheduled segment. When a beat passes, the timer moves directly to the next one, keeping the planned rhythm moving.
ETC
The local clock time at which the next target is due.

The next target number and total target for the segment, such as 2 / 5.

Recorded completions divided by the unrounded expectation earned over productive time elapsed so far.
Day / segment apps per hour
Completions divided by productive hours elapsed for the whole day or current segment.
Outcome buttons
Approve, Decline, Resub, ORE, and Other record a completion against the productive queue active in the schedule.
Day Overview
A timeline of planned segments with completed-versus-expected values, plus productive time, non-productive time, and completion count.

Schedule

Open Menu → Schedule. Set shift start and end, then use + Add activity. Choose a queue/activity and start time for each row.

  • A segment runs from its start until the next segment starts. The last segment runs until shift end.
  • The default day is 11:00–19:30 with 15 minutes of Team Briefings.
  • Each start must be inside the shift, and two activities cannot share the same start.
  • Rows are sorted chronologically. Deleting a row causes the preceding activity to run until the following row.
  • Outcome buttons use the queue in the active scheduled segment.

Close the dialog to validate it. Invalid shift bounds, out-of-range starts, or duplicate starts must be corrected first.

Import a copied day

Open Menu → Import Schedule, paste the complete day, and choose Preview import. The importer reads the date from the first row, shift bounds from the second row, and each activity name immediately followed by its time range. Location, comments, and other text are ignored.

  • Review every extracted activity before choosing Replace today’s plan.
  • Known BCA labels and Team Briefings are matched to their existing queue options.
  • An unrecognised activity is added to Queue Settings at zero apps per hour, making it non-productive until you edit its rate and department.
  • Identical repeated copies of an activity and its time are ignored automatically.
Replacing the plan: applying an import replaces all current schedule rows and clears the saved start-of-day snapshot. It does not delete completion history.

Queue settings

Open Menu → Queue Settings to create, edit, or delete queue definitions and manage departments.

Add a queue

  • Queue name: the label shown throughout the dashboard.
  • Apps per hour: the expected throughput. Decimal values are accepted. A rate of zero makes the entry non-productive and creates no app targets.
  • Department: required for productive queues. BCA and PCA are included by default; AA belongs under PCA while the initial productive queues belong under BCA.
  • Colour: identifies the queue in schedule and timeline displays.

Edit and delete

Use a queue row's controls to edit its name, rate, department, or colour. A queue cannot be deleted while today's plan uses it.

Manage departments

Add or rename departments in the Departments section. A custom department can only be deleted when it has no queues. Built-in BCA and PCA cannot be deleted.

Built-in activities: Non-productive, Off queue, and Team Briefings are locked at zero apps/hour. They cannot be renamed or deleted, although their colours can be changed.

Recording completions

During a productive scheduled segment, press an outcome button to add a timestamped completion against its active planned queue. Outcomes cannot be recorded during non-productive activities or outside a scheduled segment.

Use Menu → Completed Apps to review today’s entries, correct a completion time, change its task/queue, or delete mistakes. Changing the time automatically assigns the productive queue scheduled at the new time. If no productive queue was scheduled then, the existing queue remains selected and the entry is shown without a scheduled activity; use the task/queue dropdown to override it.

Targets and timer

A productive segment's raw expectation is its hours multiplied by its queue's apps-per-hour rate. Any positive result is rounded up to a whole app. Target due times are spread evenly from segment start to segment end.

On the Day Overview timeline, the target-adjustment control reduces a segment's rounded requirement by one; selecting it again restores the app. The result never falls below zero. This is stored with the segment and is reflected in the timer, reminders, totals and review.

The timer shows no active target before/after the shift, during zero-rate activities, or after every slot in the current segment has passed. Near the last scheduled target, a one-time Final App overlay appears for that segment.

Notification settings

Open Menu → Notifications.

Enable scheduled target reminders
Turns the periodic due-target checks on or off. Enabling reminders starts with the next target rather than replaying one that is already due.
Send test toast
Shows an immediate in-page sample.
Play a sound
Generates a short reminder tone. Browsers may require a user interaction before audio is allowed.
Play a click when recording outcomes
Plays a short click after an outcome is successfully recorded. Turn this off to keep the button animation silent.

Target reminders and the Final App overlay are paused while the Schedule dialog is open. Targets that pass while you edit are skipped, and reminders resume with the next target after the schedule closes.

Keep the tab open. Reminders are in-page toasts, so this static application cannot show them after the dashboard is closed.

Reset and date rollover

Reset Day asks for confirmation. It restores the default shift and Team Briefings and clears live day data. Queue definitions and notification preferences remain.

If the page remains open across a local calendar-date change, a dialog offers:

  • Export CSV & reset: export when completions exist, reset, and open the new schedule.
  • Reset without export: reset immediately and open the schedule.
  • Skip for now: keep the existing data, mark it as today's state, and open the schedule.
Before resetting: export the CSV if an external completion record is required. Browser data can disappear when site data is cleared.

Storage, privacy, and exports

State is stored as JSON in browser localStorage under queuePerformanceDashboard.v2. Nothing is uploaded by this app. Storage belongs to the current browser profile and origin, so local-file, localhost, different ports, different browsers, and private windows may not share data.

The CSV contains the scheduled queue, outcome, recorded time, planned queue, and planned segment. See the data dictionary for the complete schema and calculations.

Troubleshooting

No pace timer or target

Check that local time is within a scheduled productive segment, its queue rate is above zero, and a future target slot remains.

Notifications are missing

Enable reminders, keep the dashboard open, and try Send test toast. Check browser audio restrictions if the toast appears without sound.

Schedule will not close

Ensure shift end follows shift start, every activity starts inside the shift, and no two rows use the same time.

Cannot delete a queue

Built-ins are permanent. For another queue, first change or delete every current schedule segment that references it.

Previous data is missing

Return to the same browser profile and URL/origin. Check whether site data was cleared or private browsing was used. The application has no cloud recovery.

CSV will not export

There must be at least one current completion. Browser download policies can also block automatic downloads.
